Safety

Talli checks before it changes the ledger.

If a name, amount, or payment is unclear, Talli pauses and asks. Nothing changes until it has a safe answer.

  • Clear ledger updates
  • Saved history
  • No change during clarification
  • Safe failure behavior

Real-world examples

Built around the messages merchants already send.

Credit sale

"Sarah took $180 of stock."

Talli records the new credit and keeps the customer balance current.

Partial payment

"She paid $50 today."

The balance drops by the paid amount and the remaining amount stays visible.

Settlement

"She paid the rest."

The debt closes when the remaining balance is paid in full.

Correction

"Actually it was $160, not $180."

The original amount is corrected and the change stays in history.

Ambiguity

Two customers named Sarah owe money, so Talli asks before recording a payment.

Clarification is part of the product, not a failure.
